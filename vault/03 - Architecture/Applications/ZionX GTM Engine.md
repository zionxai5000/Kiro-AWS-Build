# ZionX GTM Engine

> Go-to-market automation for app launches — from research to revenue optimization.

## Purpose

Automates the full go-to-market lifecycle for ZionX apps: market research, ASO (App Store Optimization), campaign management, landing pages, portfolio management, and revenue optimization.

## Components

### Market Research (`market-research.ts`)
- Competitor analysis and niche identification
- Market sizing and opportunity scoring
- Trend detection in app categories

### ASO Engine (`aso-engine.ts`)
- Keyword research and optimization
- Title, subtitle, description optimization
- Screenshot and preview video recommendations
- Rank tracking and A/B testing

### Campaign Manager (`campaign-manager.ts`)
- Multi-channel campaign orchestration
- Budget allocation across channels
- Performance tracking and optimization
- Creative rotation

### Landing Page Generator (`landing-page-generator.ts`)
- Template-based landing page creation via Zeely driver
- A/B variant generation
- Conversion tracking setup
- Mobile-optimized outputs

### Portfolio Manager (`portfolio-manager.ts`)
- Multi-app portfolio oversight
- Cross-app promotion opportunities
- Resource allocation across apps
- Lifecycle stage tracking

### Revenue Optimizer (`revenue-optimizer.ts`)
- Pricing strategy recommendations
- In-app purchase optimization
- Subscription tier analysis
- LTV/CAC optimization
