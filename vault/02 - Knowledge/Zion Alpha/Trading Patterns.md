## Risk Framework

- Maximum position size: defined by [[Otzar]] budget allocation
- Maximum daily loss: defined by [[Otzar]] daily limit
- Never exceed: Kelly criterion fraction (quarter-Kelly for prediction markets)
- Mandatory stop-loss: exit at 2x initial risk
