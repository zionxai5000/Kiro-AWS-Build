## How Formulas Are Created

1. ZXMG analyzes reference channels/videos via [[YouTube Channel Analyzer]]
2. Patterns are extracted and tested against performance data
3. Consistent patterns become codified formulas
4. Formulas are applied to content production as quality gates
5. Performance is tracked — formulas are updated based on real results
