# Cross-Business Synergies

> Managed by [[Eretz]] Synergy Engine. Revenue, operational, and strategic synergies across all subsidiaries.

## Standing Rules (Always Enforced)

| Rule | Source | Target | Description |
|------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Video Commercials | [[ZXMG]] | [[ZionX]] | Every ZXMG video includes a ZionX app commercial |
| Market Intelligence | [[Zion Alpha]] | [[ZionX]] | Trading insights inform app opportunity identification |
| App Launch Campaigns | [[ZionX]] | [[ZXMG]] | Every app launch triggers a content campaign |
| Pattern Sharing | All | All | Success patterns extracted and made available to all subsidiaries |

## Synergy Types

### Revenue Synergies
- ZXMG videos drive ZionX app downloads (cross-promotion)
- ZionX apps referenced in ZXMG content (embedded demos)
- Zion Alpha edge detection identifies app market opportunities

### Operational Synergies
- Shared creative assets (thumbnails, graphics, branding)
- Shared audience data (what users want → inform app features)
- Shared production formulas (what works in content → apply to app marketing)

### Strategic Synergies
- Portfolio diversification reduces risk
- Cross-pollination of domain expertise
- Combined data gives better market intelligence than any single pillar
