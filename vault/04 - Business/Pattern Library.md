
## Pattern Categories

- **Growth Patterns** — User/viewer acquisition strategies that work
- **Monetization Patterns** — Revenue optimization approaches
- **Retention Patterns** — Keeping users/viewers engaged long-term
- **Operations Patterns** — Efficiency and automation wins
- **Marketing Patterns** — Promotion strategies that convert

## How Patterns Are Created

1. A subsidiary achieves measurable success with a specific approach
2. [[Eretz]] extracts the generalizable principle
3. The pattern is documented here with evidence
4. [[Eretz]] proactively recommends the pattern when other subsidiaries face related challenges
5. Effectiveness is tracked per-subsidiary
